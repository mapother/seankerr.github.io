When to call me

You have spent heavily on AI and cannot clearly explain the return.

The program may be useful. It may not be. Either way, the economics should be understandable.

Your AI initiative is not performing the way the business case promised.

You need to separate technical problems, organizational problems, vendor claims and unrealistic assumptions.

You are being asked to approve another AI investment and want a second opinion.

Before committing more capital, examine the assumptions, costs, dependencies and expected value.

You have too many pilots, vendors, tools and competing internal opinions.

Establish what exists, what matters, what does not, and which decisions actually need to be made.

Your technical team and business leadership are talking past one another.

Translate the problem into economic, operational and technical terms everyone can evaluate.

You are becoming dependent on AI without examining what happens when it is unavailable.

Identify the cloud, API, data, vendor and infrastructure dependencies now embedded in the operating model.
